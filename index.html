WorkSmart: From Paper Tallies to Transparent Harvest Operations

WorkSmart is our Python/Flask platform that digitizes farm labour and harvest management end to end.

Challenge

Daily labour output and harvest volumes were tracked on paper tallies — making payroll slow, pay disputes common, and dispatched tonnage hard to reconcile with what was harvested.

Solution

  • Labourers and drivers log daily output with photo proof of work.
  • Admins verify each entry; earnings, payouts and per-property progress are calculated automatically.
  • Every correction is recorded with a reason in a full audit trail.
  • Outbound truck loads are logged with receipt proof, so harvested and dispatched tonnage can be reconciled.
  • Crop, soil and irrigation data feed yield-per-acre insights.

Impact

  • Faster payroll
  • Fewer pay disputes
  • Clearer visibility into field profitability

It runs securely on a lightweight cloud stack with JWT authentication, role-based access and built-in monitoring.

FormBuddy: Ending Repetitive Data Entry for Commercial Insurance Agents

An extension-based web application for commercial business insurance agents.

Challenge

To quote a commercial policy, agents re-type the same customer details into one insurance carrier portal after another — slow, repetitive and easy to get wrong.

Solution

  • Agents capture customer information once and retain it securely.
  • The browser extension prefills that information into other insurance companies' portals.
  • An agent portal lets agents fully manage the portfolios they work on and track the commissions they earn.

Impact

  • Enter customer data once, reuse it everywhere
  • Faster quoting across multiple carriers
  • Fewer re-keying errors
  • One view of portfolios and commissions
